# ERA Integrated Project Management System (ERA-IPMS)

## Project Concept Note

**Project Title:** ERA Integrated Project Management System (ERA-IPMS)
**Project Type:** Web-Based Information and Project Management System
**Initial Pilot Organisation:** Emergency Response Aid (ERA)
**System Owner and Developer:** Abdullahi Abdi Mohamed
**Current Version:** Concept and Planning Stage
**Date:** September 2026


## 1. Background

Emergency Response Aid (ERA) is a Community-Based Organisation involved in disability services, community advocacy and awareness, poultry farming, and small-scale farming activities.

ERA provides disability-related services including home visits, assessments, referrals, follow-up support, and capacity-building activities. Community volunteers, ERA members, and field staff may participate in delivering and supporting these services.

ERA also manages livelihood activities, including a small poultry project and a small farm. The poultry project currently focuses mainly on egg production and includes local and Kienyeji chickens. ERA plans to expand the poultry project in the future to support additional activities such as meat production and breeding.

The small farm grows bananas, local or natural vegetables, and sukuma wiki. Farm activities include planting, watering, weeding, and harvesting. Farm produce can support the poultry project as feed, while future surplus produce may also be sold.

Currently, much of ERA's information is managed using manual and paper-based documentation. ERA-IPMS is proposed as a centralised digital system to improve the management, monitoring, and reporting of these activities.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

ERA currently relies primarily on manual, paper-based documentation for managing beneficiary, activity, poultry, farm, financial, and organisational records.

This creates several challenges. Finding old beneficiary or activity records can be difficult, while paper documents may be lost, damaged, or duplicated. Preparing reports is time-consuming, and it can be difficult to determine the total number of beneficiaries served.

ERA also faces challenges in tracking referrals and follow-up activities, monitoring poultry information such as chicken numbers, egg production, feed, illnesses, and deaths, and monitoring farm production and expenses.

The current manual system also makes it more difficult to produce consistent and high-quality reports for management, projects, and other organisational purposes.


## 3. Project Goal

The goal of ERA-IPMS is to develop a centralised web-based information and project management system that improves the recording, management, monitoring, analysis, and reporting of ERA's disability services, organisational activities, poultry project, and small farm.

## 4. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Digitise selected manual records currently used by ERA.
2. Improve the registration and management of beneficiary information.
3. Support the recording and tracking of disability assessments, home visits, referrals, and follow-ups.
4. Improve monitoring of poultry numbers, egg production, feed, illnesses, deaths, and related activities.
5. Introduce structured digital records for small farm activities, crops, harvests, expenses, and produce transfers to poultry.
6. Support the management of projects and organisational activities.
7. Improve the management of staff and volunteer information.
8. Support the recording of expenses and financial information.
9. Provide management with a central dashboard containing key organisational information.
10. Improve the speed, consistency, and quality of organisational and project reporting.

## 5. Proposed Solution

ERA-IPMS will be developed as a web-based information management system.

The system will provide authorised users with a central platform for recording and managing organisational information.

The proposed system will initially include the following major modules:

### 5.1 Disability and Beneficiary Management

This module will support:

* Beneficiary registration.
* Disability assessments.
* Home visit records.
* Referrals.
* Referral tracking.
* Assignment of volunteers or staff for follow-up.
* Follow-up records.
* Recording of follow-up outcomes.
* Quick searching of beneficiary records.

### 5.2 Project and Activity Management

This module will support:

* Project registration and management.
* Activity planning.
* Recording completed activities.
* Tracking upcoming activities.
* Tracking pending activities.
* Activity information for reporting.

### 5.3 Poultry Management

This module will support:

* Poultry or flock records.
* Recording chicken numbers.
* Recording the purchase of chickens or chicks.
* Recording poultry deaths.
* Recording poultry illnesses.
* Recording egg production.
* Recording feed usage.
* Recording feed received from the small farm.
* Recording purchased feed.
* Recording poultry-related expenses.
* Assigning poultry-related tasks and responsibilities.

### 5.4 Small Farm Management

This module will support:

* Recording crops.
* Recording planting activities.
* Recording watering and weeding activities.
* Recording harvests.
* Recording farm expenses.
* Recording produce transferred to the poultry project.
* Supporting future recording of farm produce sales and income.

### 5.5 Staff and Volunteer Management

The system will support:

* Staff records.
* Volunteer records.
* User roles.
* User access levels.
* Assignment of responsibilities where required.

### 5.6 Finance Management

The initial finance features will support the recording and management of:

* Organisational expenses.
* Project expenses.
* Poultry expenses.
* Farm expenses.
* Other relevant financial records.

### 5.7 Dashboard and Reporting

The system dashboard should provide management with access to important information, including:

* Total beneficiaries.
* Number of home visits.
* Number of referrals.
* Referral follow-up and completion status.
* Current poultry numbers.
* Egg production.
* Feed usage and purchases.
* Poultry illnesses and deaths.
* Farm crops and harvests.
* Farm expenses.
* Organisational and project expenses.
* Number of activities conducted.
* Number of staff and volunteers.
* Upcoming activities.
* Pending activities.

The system should also support report generation and future export to PDF and Excel formats.

## 6. Target Users

* Field Staff.

* Finance Personnel.
* Monitoring and Evaluation Personnel.

## 7. Current Information Management Process

ERA currently uses a combination of manual paper-based records.

Existing records include:

* Home visit forms.
* Activity reports.
* Poultry records.
* Expense records.
* Financial records.
* Meeting minutes.
* Staff and volunteer records.
* Beneficiary registers.
* Project reports.

Some important information areas do not currently have formal records, including structured digital disability assessments, referral records, attendance records, egg production records, feed records, and farm activity and harvest records.

ERA-IPMS will address both the digitisation of existing records and the introduction of structured records where gaps currently exist.

## 8. Current Disability Service Workflow

The current disability support process generally follows this structure:

```text
Person with Disability Identified
        ↓
Assessment Conducted
        ↓
Information Recorded
        ↓
Needs Identified
        ↓
Referral to Appropriate Service
        ↓
Referral Through Verbal Communication,
Accompaniment, or Written Referral
        ↓
Volunteer Follow-Up
        ↓
Outcome Confirmed
```

The future system will provide a structured method for recording and tracking these stages.

## 9. Poultry and Small Farm Relationship

ERA's small farm and poultry activities are connected.

The small farm produces crops and vegetables that can support the poultry project. Poultry feed may therefore come from:

1. Produce from the small farm.
2. Purchased local feed.

The system should be designed to record this relationship.

```text
Small Farm
    ↓
Farm Produce
    ↓
Used to Support Poultry
```

In the future, the farm may also support income generation through the sale of surplus produce.

## 13. Data Protection and Confidentiality

ERA-IPMS may manage beneficiary and organisational information that requires appropriate protection.

During development:

* Real beneficiary data will not be uploaded to the public GitHub repository.
* Fictional or anonymised data will be used for testing.
* Passwords and credentials will not be committed to the repository.
* Environment variables and secret files will be excluded using `.gitignore`.
* Confidential organisational records will not be publicly published.

Data access within the system will eventually be controlled through user roles and permissions.

## 17. Conclusion

ERA-IPMS is proposed as a practical digital solution to improve how ERA manages information related to disability services, community activities, poultry farming, and small-scale farming.

The development process will proceed incrementally, beginning with detailed planning and requirements analysis before database design and software development.
